check_graph_t_junction_gaps(_road_codes text[], _options jsonb)


Description

Check G02 run by the function check_graph: find the edges ending near another edge without being connected to it, for example a road arriving on another road and stopping a few decimeters before it (T junction).

An issue is returned for each free end of an edge (no other edge is connected to it) which is less than the tolerance from another edge: option tolerance, 2 m by default. The edges connected to the opposite end of the edge are ignored, for example its next edge if it is very short, and the edges crossed by the edge near its free end: the edge goes beyond the other edge, which is found by the check G01. When two free ends are close to each other, only one issue is returned.

The point to join is the closest point of the other edge, or its start or end point if it is less than 50 cm from it. The details give the road codes of the two edges, the distance, and the join type: edge or edge_end. The issue can be fixed with the action extend_edge, which adds the point to join at the free end of the edge.
