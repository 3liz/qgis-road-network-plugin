check_graph_reversed_edges(_road_codes text[], _options jsonb)


Description

Check G05 run by the function check_graph: find the edges in the opposite direction of their road.

The edges of a road are grouped in sequences: two edges follow each other at a node where there are only these two edges of the road. The direction of each sequence is given by the order of the markers of the road located along it (less than the option marker_tolerance from it, 10 m by default), or else by the direction of most of the length of its edges. The markers of the roundabouts are not used. The direction of the edges is given by their geometry, not by their start and end nodes.

An issue is returned for each group of consecutive edges in the opposite direction of their sequence. The details give the reason (markers or neighbours), the number of edges and their length, and the number of consecutive markers in the direction of the road and in the opposite direction. The issue can be fixed with the action reverse_edges, which reverses the geometry of the edges.
